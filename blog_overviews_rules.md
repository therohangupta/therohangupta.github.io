
# blog rules


In my blog, I have a few different types of things I'll write about. Read therohangupta.github.io/_posts/2026-03-31-welcome-to-my-blog.md to get an idea of what I will. 

I want all my blog posts to acknowledge that they are written with the help of AI but in a smug kind of "of course he used it get with the times" kind of vibe but also still acknowledging I guided and proofread and really was the architect behind the originality and stuff like that. This is what every blog post should say: "**Note**: I used AI while writing this, because it is 2026 and refusing good tools is not a personality trait. The ideas, structure, edits, and final judgment are mine; AI helped me explore, pressure-test, and sharpen the writing." before the table of contents. 


## idea dump blog type rules (specific category: idea dump)
So for my blog, I am planning to have a sporadic or maybe a consistent-ish series of blog posts called "Idea Dump" which can be just any cool ideas that maybe relate to ML or some other fields. They can be ideas from papers, cool product ideas, some observations on some field, etc. Basically very broad. But the unifying thread is that they will be ideas that I find interesting and maybe others will too. 

So I want those to start with a clear intro that maintains my voice that says something quick like "my latest idea dump about papers, trends, product ideas, and more. This dump includes: [idea #1, idea #2, idea #3, etc.]"

So far I have no idea dumps but will draft something soon so don't have to care about those right now.
