Welcome! This is my personal blog where I'll be writing about anything that piques my interest in AI, machine learning, and robotics. If you're here, you're probably curious about similar topics, and I'm glad you stumbled upon my blog!

## Why I'm Writing

I'm starting this blog for a simple reason: **writing helps me clarify concepts and ideas in ways I can understand**. There's something powerful about the process of translating thoughts into words as it forces you to confront gaps in your understanding, to trace connections between ideas, and to build mental models that actually make sense.

When I'm confused about something — whether it's a foundational concept, a recent research paper, or a cutting-edge technique — I find that the best way to resolve that confusion is to write about it. Not just summarize it, but to work through the steps and abstractions needed to truly understand it, no matter how many steps that takes.

## What to Expect

This blog will cover a wide range of topics:

- **Foundational concepts**: Sometimes I'll write about fundamental ideas in AI/ML — things like attention mechanisms, backpropagation, or reinforcement learning basics — explained in my own way, from first principles when needed.

- **Recent trends**: Other posts will dive into cutting-edge research, new architectures, or emerging techniques that are shaping the field.

- **Opinion pieces**: Some posts will be a snapshot of my opinions at a given point in time on a specific idea or method or general topic in the space.

- **My own work**: Occasionally, I'll write about projects I'm working on or research I'm involved in, aiming for a broader audience of researchers and enthusiasts.

- **Paper thoughts**: I'll share summaries and analyses of papers that catch my attention, breaking down complex ideas into digestible pieces.

- **Projects**: I'll document interesting projects I'm building, the challenges I face, and what I learn along the way.

## My Approach

My general approach to writing is straightforward: **clarify things in terms that I can understand first, and hopefully others — especially those new to the field — can understand too**.

This isn't meant to be an educational blog per se, but many posts will naturally explain things because that's how I learn. I'm not interested in just collecting facts; I want to understand the abstractions, the connections, and the reasoning behind ideas.

When I encounter something confusing, I don't just want to know *what* it is – I want to understand *why* it works, *how* it fits into the bigger picture, and *what* the key insights are that make it click.

## On Using AI

Yes, I'll use AI tools to help me write these posts. Here's why: **AI helps me understand the ideas I want to write about too**. It's a tool for exploration, for asking questions, for testing my understanding, and for finding connections I might have missed. The goal isn't to have AI write for me — it's to use AI as a thinking partner in the process of understanding and explaining complex topics.

## Who This Is For

Most posts will be written with an audience similar to me in mind — people who are curious, who want to understand things deeply, and who appreciate clear explanations even when the topics are complex. Some posts will be more technical, some more accessible, but all will aim for clarity.

If you're new to AI/ML/robotics, I hope some of these posts help you understand concepts that might otherwise seem intimidating. If you're more experienced, I hope you find interesting perspectives or connections you hadn't considered.

## What's Next

I don't have a strict posting schedule — I'll write when something captures my interest or when I've worked through an idea enough to share it. The topics will be whatever I'm curious about at the moment, which means the blog will naturally evolve with my interests.

Thanks for reading, and I hope you find something interesting here. If you have questions, thoughts, or suggestions, feel free to reach out. Let's explore these ideas together.
