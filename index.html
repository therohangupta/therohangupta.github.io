Rohan Gupta

Hi! I'm Rohan. I'm currently a Senior Machine Learning Engineer at Synopsys, where I apply ML to chip design. I also interned there and helped build intelligent LLM-based agents for existing products and tools.

I completed my undergraduate and masters in Computer Science at the University of Southern California, where I worked in the GLAMOR Lab on multimodal AI and robotics, focusing on policy evaluation and single and multi-robot planning and learning.

During my time at USC, I received the merit-based Presidential Scholarship and won the Provost's Research Fellowship award for my work in GLAMOR. I continue to stay involved with the lab's research efforts as an alumni. I also was part of a band.

Whether they're embodied or purely virtual, I hope to be part of building the next wave of robust AI systems in my career.
